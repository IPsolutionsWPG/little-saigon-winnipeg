Our story

More Than a Meal. A Little Taste of Home.

At Little Saigon, we believe good food brings people together. Our family-run restaurant brings Vietnamese flavours, comforting favourites and warm hospitality to Winnipeg's Exchange District.

The room is casual and family-friendly, with generous portions, plenty of fresh herbs at the table, and a substantial selection of vegetarian and vegan options. Come for a steaming bowl, stay for an iced coffee.
