About

A family-run table in the Exchange

Little Saigon Restaurant is a family-run Vietnamese eatery in Winnipeg’s Exchange District, known for pho, crispy chicken wings and regional specialties — plus an attached specialty coffee counter, Saigon Grind.

The room is casual and family-friendly, with generous portions, plenty of fresh herbs at the table, and a substantial selection of vegetarian and vegan options. Come for a steaming bowl, stay for an iced coffee.

Good to know: rear parking is available, and Thursday is Wing Night — wings for $11.99 with a specialty drink.
